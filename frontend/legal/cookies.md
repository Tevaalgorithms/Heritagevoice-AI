A cookie is a small file a website stores in your browser.

## What we use

HeritageVoice AI uses **only the cookies it needs to work**:

| Cookie | What it does | When it is set | How long |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sign-in session | Keeps you signed in as you move between pages | Only after you sign in | Until you sign out or the session ends |
| Sign-in provider | Set by our sign-in provider (Amazon Cognito) while you type your password and set up multi-factor authentication | Only on the sign-in page | A short time |

Searching the public collection does **not** set any cookie.

## What we do not use

We do not use advertising, tracking or analytics cookies, and we do not share cookie information with
anyone. Because we only use cookies that are strictly necessary, we do not ask you to accept them.

## Your choices

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. If you block the sign-in cookies you will
not be able to sign in, but you can still search and read what is public.
