This notice explains what personal information HeritageVoice AI collects, why, who sees it and what
your choices are. The service is run by **{{operator}}** (the "controller"). Contact: **{{contact}}**.

## What we collect

| Who | What | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Everyone who searches | The words you search for are used to answer the request. We do not build a profile of searches, but they can appear in short-lived server logs together with your network address and a request number. Your network address is also used briefly to limit how often one visitor can ask. | To run the service and keep it safe |
| People with an account | Email address, a password (held by our sign-in provider, never by us), your role and community, the date you accepted these terms, and a record of what you upload or change. | To sign you in, to keep a record of who did what, and to protect communities |
| Contributors | Recordings (a voice is personal information), their transcripts and translations, the language, the community and the consent record that goes with each recording. | To keep and share heritage as the community agreed |
| Reviewers and administrators | Review decisions and administrative actions, with the date. | Accountability |

We do **not** use advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not sell personal information.

## Who handles it for us

| Provider | What it does | What it sees |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Amazon Web Services | Hosting, storage, backups, sign-in (Cognito) and speech-to-text (Transcribe) | Everything stored on the service, including audio |
| Language-model providers (for example Anthropic, OpenAI) | Translate transcripts and write answers | The **text** of transcripts or passages needed for a request, not audio |
| Pinecone | Turns text into search vectors and stores the vectors | Passage text while vectors are computed; vectors are stored without the text |

Data is stored in the **United States** (AWS region us-west-2) and processed by these providers, who
act for us under their own terms and privacy policies. We send them only what a request needs.

## How long we keep it

Recordings and their text are kept until the community withdraws consent or an administrator removes
them. Account details are kept while the account exists. Server logs are kept for a short period for
security and fault-finding. Encrypted backups are kept for a limited time and then deleted.
Withdrawing consent removes material from the service and from search straight away.

## Your choices and rights

You may ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, limit or object
to how we use it, or move it to someone else, and you may withdraw consent you have given. If you
contributed on behalf of a community, the community's decisions about its recordings come first. Write
to **{{contact}}**. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

## Protecting information

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Sign-in uses multi-factor authentication. Access is limited
by role and community, checked on every request, and changes by administrators are recorded. No
system is perfectly secure; if there is a breach that affects you we will tell you as the law requires.

## Children

The service is not aimed at children. Do not include children's names or personal details in
recordings unless a parent, guardian or the community has agreed.

## Cookies

We use **only the cookies needed to work**: a sign-in session cookie (set only after you sign in) and
a short-lived one from our sign-in provider, Amazon Cognito, while you sign in. Searching the public
collection sets no cookie. We use no advertising, tracking or analytics cookies, so we do not ask you
to accept any. You can block cookies in your browser; you can then still search, but not sign in.
