## 1. What the service is

HeritageVoice AI helps communities keep their songs, stories, lessons, practices and other living
knowledge in their own voices and languages. Contributors record or upload material. Each recording
comes with a record of what its community has agreed to. An administrator reviews it before anyone can
search it. Computers help to transcribe and translate it, and to find and summarise passages.

## 2. Reading and searching

source as shown. You may not copy the collection in bulk, republish it, sell it, or use it to train
or improve a machine-learning model without our written permission and the permission of the
communities concerned.

## 5. Community control and cultural care

## 6. Computer-generated content

Transcripts, translations, summaries and answers are produced with the help of computer models and
may be wrong or incomplete, especially for less common languages, names and songs. Administrators
review material before it is searchable, but we cannot promise it is accurate. Answers always show
the passages they come from, so you can check them. Do not rely on them for decisions that matter
without checking the original recording or asking the community.
