We want everyone to be able to use HeritageVoice AI, including people who use screen readers, keyboards
only, magnification, or who have limited vision, hearing, movement or cognitive differences.

## Our aim

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA. We have **not yet had
the service formally audited**, so we cannot say it fully conforms.

## What we have done

- Text is a readable size and has strong contrast against its background.
- Pages work with a keyboard, and buttons and fields have clear labels and visible focus.
- Everything is written in plain language and works on phones and large screens.
- Passages are shown as text, in the original language and in English translation.

## Known limits

- The service is built with a tool that does not give us full control of every control's markup, so
  some screen-reader announcements may be less clear than we would like.
- Audio and video playback is not offered yet. When it is, transcripts will be available alongside.
- Machine translation may not read naturally in every language.
- The sign-in page is provided by our sign-in provider; we can change how it looks but not all of how
  it works.
