This policy is part of the [Terms of Use](/terms). It helps keep the service safe and respectful for
the communities who trust it with their heritage.

## Please do

- Credit the source shown with each passage when you quote it.
- Respect what each community has chosen to share and what it has not.
- Tell us when something looks wrong, sensitive or unlawful.

## Please do not

- **Copy in bulk.** Do not scrape, crawl or download the collection, or use automated tools to search
  faster than a person would. The service limits how often one visitor can ask.
- **Train or tune models** on the collection, or build another dataset from it, without written
  permission from us and the communities concerned.
- **Share what you should not.** Do not upload recordings you have no right to, material the speakers
  have not agreed to, private details about people who have not agreed, or content that is unlawful,
  threatening, hateful, or that harasses anyone.
- **Misrepresent.** Do not present transcripts, translations or answers as the words or the
  authority of a community or elder, or use remedies as medical advice.
- **Harm the service.** Do not try to get around sign-in, access limits or rate limits, probe for
  weaknesses, or disrupt the service. If you find a security problem, tell us at **{{contact}}** and
  give us reasonable time to fix it before you share it.
- **Pretend.** Do not use another person's account or pretend to be someone you are not.

## What may happen

We may remove content, limit access, or suspend or close an account that breaks this policy, and we
may report unlawful activity to the authorities. Contact **{{contact}}** to appeal.
