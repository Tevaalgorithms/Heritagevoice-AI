## Asking for removal or restriction

You can ask us to remove or restrict something at any time if you are a speaker, a contributor, a
member or leader of the community concerned, or you believe content is unlawful, harmful or sensitive.

Please tell us:

1. **What**: the recording, or the passage and when you saw it.
2. **Who you are** and your connection to it (for example, "I am the speaker" or "I am a member of
   the community").
3. **What you want**: remove it, hide it from the public, or something else.
4. **Why**, if you wish to say.

You do not need to be a lawyer. We will acknowledge your request promptly, tell you who is looking at
it, and tell you what we decided and why. Where a community has withdrawn its consent, the recording is
taken out of view and out of search straight away, before any discussion.

## Cultural sensitivity

If a community leader tells us that some knowledge is sacred, secret or only for certain people, we
will restrict it first and talk afterwards.

## Reporting a security problem

Please write to the address above with details. We ask that you give us reasonable time to fix a
problem before sharing it, and we will not take action against someone who reports in good faith and
does not harm the service or people's information.
