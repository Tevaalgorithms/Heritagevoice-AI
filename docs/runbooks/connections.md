# Connections: search, English and voice

The Connections page (open to visitors and signed-in readers) shows relationships a person approved,
each with the words in the recording that say it.

## Getting a relationship onto the page
1. A recording is published (transcript and translation approved).
2. The model proposes relationships; **Administration → Relationships → To review** shows them. To
   ask again for a recording already published, use **Find relationships again**.
3. In the form, check **From**, **Relationship** and **To** (in the speaker's language), and the two
   **In English** boxes. Approve.
4. To add or fix English meanings later, open an approved item and use **English meanings**.

## Searching
- Type a name or idea and press **Search**. It matches the speaker's words and the English meanings,
  so both *பணிவு* and *humility* find the same connection.
- **Search by voice**: pick the language, press the microphone, say a few words and stop. The words fill the
  box. Clips are limited to 15 seconds; the recording is deleted as soon as it is heard and nothing
  about it is kept.
- The Home page search has the same **Speak instead** box. The heard words appear in the words box
  by themselves; the person then presses Search. Same endpoints and limits. (On Connections the
  search also runs by itself.)
- Typed and spoken text is tidied first: punctuation and sounds like "hmm" are ignored, so
  "பணிவு." finds what "பணிவு" finds.
- Limits (each use costs a transcription): signed-in readers 6 a minute; a visitor 3 a minute; all
  visitors together 20 a minute. Beyond that the page asks them to wait.

## English
Each connection has an **English** switch. It shows the sentence with the English meanings and the
approved English translation of the words, when both exist; otherwise it says there is no English
version yet. English meanings are part of the relationship, so a name that is edited loses its old
meaning until a new one is given.
