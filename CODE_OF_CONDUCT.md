# Code of Conduct

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1, which is licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

## Our pledge

We want everyone to take part in this project free from harassment, whatever their age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality,
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

This project works with the voices and cultural knowledge of communities. We also pledge to treat
that material, and the people who share it, with respect: no mocking, extracting or exploiting
what communities have entrusted to us.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps: being kind and patient; respecting different viewpoints and experiences;
giving and accepting constructive feedback; taking responsibility for mistakes; and putting the
community's wishes about its own material first.

Behaviour that is not acceptable: sexualised language or attention; trolling, insults or personal
attacks; harassment in public or private; publishing others' private information without
permission (including recordings or personal details); and anything else that would reasonably be
considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit or
reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and
may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they judge harmful.

This code applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers using GitHub's private reporting for
this repository (Security tab, "Report a vulnerability" is for security issues only) or by
contacting an owner listed in [`.github/CODEOWNERS`](.github/CODEOWNERS) through their GitHub
profile. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

Maintainers follow these steps: a private correction for a first minor problem; a warning for a
repeated or more serious one; a temporary ban for a sustained problem; a permanent ban for a
pattern of violations or serious harm.
